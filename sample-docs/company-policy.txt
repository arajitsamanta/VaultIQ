ACME CORP — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & BENEFITS SUMMARY (2026)

1. Working Hours & Flexible Work
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday. 
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST. 
Employees are eligible for hybrid remote work up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accruing at 1.67 days per month worked.
Unused PTO up to a maximum of 5 days may be rolled over into the following calendar year.
Any rollover balance must be utilized prior to March 31st of that year.

3. Health and Wellness Stipend
Each eligible full-time team member receives an annual health and wellness stipend of $1,200 ($100/month).
This stipend can be applied toward gym memberships, mental health apps, ergonomic home office equipment, or fitness trackers.

4. Learning and Development
Acme Corp provides up to $2,500 annually per employee for approved educational courses, technical certifications, and conference attendance.
Tuition reimbursement requests must be submitted through the People Portal at least 14 days prior to course registration.

5. Technology & Security Policy
All company data must remain encrypted at rest and in transit.
Employees must use hardware security keys (FIDO2/WebAuthn) for multi-factor authentication across all internal single sign-on (SSO) systems.
Customer confidential data must never be pasted into unapproved public AI tools.
